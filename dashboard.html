    Clinical Pathway & Round Simulator

    Clinical care pathways allow attending physicians and nursing staff to initialize standardized encounter trajectories and bedside observation rounds. Patient encounter updates persist across session storage.

    Protocol A: Bedside Evening Observation Round Appends a PM observation round at current timestamp with stable vital signs and continued standard of care without modification to standing physician orders.
    Protocol B: Multi-Day Inpatient Clinical Pathway (CAP Resolution) Populates complete inpatient trajectory: Day 1 ER Admission (orders), Day 2 Morning Rounds (antibiotic shift), Day 2 Afternoon Review (observation), and Day 3 Discharge Planning.
